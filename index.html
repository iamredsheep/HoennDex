A bulk lot is a flat-fee buy (e.g. 100 cards for $180) whose cost is split equally per card ($1.80 each). Click Open on a lot, then Sell a Card to name a card and record its sale — it shows as sold here and flows into your Sales, Dashboard, and per-show totals with the split cost as COGS.

Net Profit = sales gross profit (revenue − cost of cards sold − fees) − table rent − expenses tagged to this show. Cash Flow = cash collected from sales − cash spent on buys, table rent, and show expenses (how much your cash changed at the event). Tag a Sale, Purchase, or Expense to a show using the “Show / Event” field on each of those.
